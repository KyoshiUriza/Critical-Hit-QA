Lv. Rank Star-Dust Lore

How to earn Star-Dust

  • +8 per correct quiz answer · +2 per incorrect (participation).
  • +10 × severity per defect caught in Bug Bounty (critical = 5, high = 3, medium = 2, low = 1).
  • +25 per test case drafted in the Test Case Builder.
  • +25 per bug report drafted in the Bug Report Builder.
  • +40 per study plan day marked complete.
  • +5 per day of your current streak (bonus caps at 30 days).
