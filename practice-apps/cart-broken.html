Shopping Cart (Buggy build)

Same feature as the clean cart — this build has real calculation and validation bugs. Money bugs are the fun ones.

Testing charter — Do the math yourself. Compare shown totals against expected. Try coupon variations, quantity boundaries, and the checkout flow with an empty cart.

Cart

Cart is empty.

Behavior spec (expected)

  • Products: Widget ($10), Gadget ($25), Gizmo ($5.99), Doohickey ($99.99).
  • Add / +/− adjust quantity; qty must always be ≥ 1.
  • Tax = 8% of the discounted subtotal (NOT of raw subtotal).
  • Free shipping when discounted subtotal ≥ $50; otherwise $5.99 flat.
  • Coupons: SAVE10 = 10%, HALFOFF = 50%, EXPIRED = rejected. Case-insensitive.
  • Money always shown to 2 decimals.
  • Checkout requires at least one item.

Answer key

  1. Tax computed on raw subtotal instead of discounted subtotal. Apply SAVE10 to $45 → tax should be $3.24 (8% of $40.50) but shows $3.60 (8% of $45).
  2. Free-shipping threshold uses raw subtotal, not discounted. Cart at $52 raw, HALFOFF applied → discounted $26, shipping should be $5.99 but shows FREE.
  3. Coupon comparison is case-sensitive. save10 is rejected as invalid; only uppercase works.
  4. Money not consistently rounded. Some lines display floating-point garbage like $17.970000000000002.
  5. Empty cart can be checked out. Clicking Checkout with 0 items still shows the success message.
  6. "−" (decrement) can push qty below 1. Item stays at qty 0 in the cart (as an active line) instead of being removed at 1→0.
  7. Coupon not cleared after checkout. Next order still shows the previous discount applied silently.
