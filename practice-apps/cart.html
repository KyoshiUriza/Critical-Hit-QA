Cart is empty.

Behavior spec

  • Products: Widget ($10), Gadget ($25), Gizmo ($5.99), Doohickey ($99.99).
  • Add to cart increments quantity; +/− adjust; qty must be ≥ 1.
  • Tax: 8% of subtotal after discount.
  • Shipping: free over $50 subtotal, else $5.99 flat.
  • Coupons: SAVE10 = 10% off, HALFOFF = 50% off (case-insensitive), EXPIRED = rejected.
  • Only one coupon at a time. Applying replaces the previous.
  • Empty cart cannot check out.

Test ideas

  • Floating-point precision (add 3× $5.99).
  • Very large quantity (999999).
  • Coupon in lowercase, with whitespace, with special chars.
  • Remove-last-item — does the empty message reappear?
