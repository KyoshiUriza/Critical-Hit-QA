Bug report quality checklist

  • Title is specific: what happens, where, under what condition.
  • Steps are minimal, deterministic, numbered — someone else can reproduce blindly.
  • Environment covers browser, OS, and the exact build/commit.
  • Expected vs. actual are separate — no burying the "should have" in prose.
  • Evidence attached: screenshot at minimum, log/HAR for backend bugs.
  • Severity and priority are separate judgments with a one-line reason each.
  • Frequency is quantified ("3 of 5 tries", not "sometimes").
  • Regression? — if it worked in a prior build, name it. Saves hours in triage.
