What to measure, and against what

SymptomHow to confirm itThe rule it breaks
Screen scrolls sideways Compare scrollWidth to clientWidth, then find the widest child WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow — no 2D scrolling at 320px equivalent
A control is missing Search the DOM before believing it. Present-but-clipped and absent are different bugs Not a WCAG failure by itself — a functional one
Hard to tap Measure the box, not the icon. Padding counts; a 20px glyph in a 44px button is fine WCAG 2.2 2.5.8 (24×24, AA). Platform guidance is stricter
Barely any content visible Header height as a percentage of viewport height, in landscape No numeric rule — argue it with the measurement
Input lost Type, rotate, look. Then check whether the re-render or a reload caused it Data loss. Severity is high regardless of the standard

Rotation is the one most teams never test, and on a real device it can also mean the on-screen keyboard appearing, the address bar collapsing, or the OS killing and restoring your activity — all of which look like a rotation and none of which are.
