Why the opener row matters. A link with target="_blank" and no rel="noopener" hands the opened page a live handle to the one that opened it. That page can then navigate its opener somewhere else — a real phishing technique, and invisible unless you look for it. Modern browsers imply noopener for target="_blank"; older ones and non-browser clients do not, so it is still worth asserting.

This page never actually touches its opener. It only reports whether it could.
