Meridian Bank (clean build)

Retail banking: transfers between accounts, a filterable statement, and a step-up confirmation. Money is the domain where rounding, boundaries and state all matter at once.

Testing charter — money is the highest-severity domain there is, so test it like one. Try transfers at and past the balance, at zero and below, to the same account, with more than two decimal places, and with the session limit already spent. Then check the statement actually reflects what you did. Every figure on this page is derived from one integer-pence ledger — if you can make the header total disagree with the statement, that is a critical find.
