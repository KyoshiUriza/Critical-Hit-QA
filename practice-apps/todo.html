    Behavior spec

    • Empty or whitespace-only input cannot be added.
    • Max 120 chars per todo.
    • Clicking a checkbox toggles complete/incomplete.
    • × button deletes a todo (no confirmation).
    • Filters: All / Active / Completed.
    • "X items left" reflects incomplete count.
    • Items persist in localStorage under practice_todos.

    Test ideas

    • Add 100+ items — does rendering still perform?
    • XSS: try adding <img src=x onerror=alert(1)>.
    • Refresh — does state survive?
    • Open in two tabs — do they diverge?
    • Keyboard-only add/toggle/delete.
