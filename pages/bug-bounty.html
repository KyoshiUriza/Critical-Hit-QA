Bug Bounty

Test the buggy practice apps, tick off each defect you find, then score yourself against the seeded catalog.

How this works

  1. Open one of the buggy apps (link at the top of each card below).
  2. Run an exploratory session — try to break every field, every calculation, every state transition.
  3. Tick finds as they happen: every buggy app has a 🐛 Bug Bounty tab on its right edge that slides out this checklist, so you never have to leave the app. Titles stay hidden until you tick — no spoilers.
  4. This page stays the overview: score across all apps, reveal the ones you missed, or clear and re-run.
  5. Your finds are saved per profile — come back tomorrow and finish the ones you missed.
