Bug Bounty

Test the buggy practice apps, tick off each defect you find, then score yourself against the seeded catalog.

How this works

  1. Open one of the buggy apps in a new tab (link at the top of each card below).
  2. Run an exploratory session — try to break every field, every calculation, every state transition. Take notes.
  3. Come back here and tick every defect you found. Titles are intentionally not revealed until you tick or reveal.
  4. Hit Score to see how many you caught, weighted by severity.
  5. Your finds are saved per browser — come back tomorrow and finish the ones you missed.
