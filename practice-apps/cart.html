    Payment

    Nothing leaves this page. There is no network here — the whole site runs with connect-src 'none'. Do not type a real card number anywhere, including practice apps.

    Behavior spec

    • Catalogue: 10 products across Hardware, Apparel and Packs. The four original SKUs keep their prices — Widget ($10), Gadget ($25), Gizmo ($5.99), Doohickey ($99.99).
    • Out-of-stock products cannot be added.
    • Add to basket increments quantity; +/− adjust; reaching 0 removes the line.
    • Tax: 8% of subtotal after discount.
    • Shipping: free over $50 after discount, else $5.99 flat.
    • Coupons: SAVE10 = 10% off, HALFOFF = 50% off (case-insensitive), EXPIRED = rejected.
    • Only one coupon at a time. Applying replaces the previous.
    • An empty basket cannot enter checkout.
    • Checkout is four steps. Delivery requires name, address and postcode; payment requires 12–19 digits.
    • Placing an order clears the basket, the coupon and the coupon message.

    Test ideas

    • Floating-point precision (add 3× $5.99 and read every money figure).
    • Very large quantity — hold the + button.
    • Coupon in lowercase, with whitespace, with special characters.
    • Remove the last item — does the empty state come back?
    • Filter to a category with nothing in stock, with "in stock only" ticked.
    • Enter checkout, go back, change the basket, go forward again.
    • Apply a coupon, place an order, then start a second one.
    • Sort by price and check the order is numeric, not lexicographic.
